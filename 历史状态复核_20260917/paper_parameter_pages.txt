4 Evaluation Metrics and
Model Interpretability
This section presents the evaluation criteria used
to assess the predictive performance and inter-
pretability of the proposed BFNE-Net framework.
Since the model addresses both trend classifica-
tion and price regression, separate performance
measures are used for the two tasks. All evalua-
tions are conducted using a five-fold chronological
time-series validation scheme, so that the valida-
tion block in each fold occurs strictly after the
corresponding training block. This preserves the
temporal ordering of the observations and reduces
the risk of look-ahead bias.
